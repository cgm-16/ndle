### Decisions made during execution

- **The band is `min-height: 150px`, not `height`.** A fixed height would let
  content taller than 150 px overlap the rack without growing the page, so
  the fit sweep would pass a broken screen. Once every phase measures 150 px
  or less, the two behave the same.
- **The frame is one token.** `--arena-max-width` is `393px`, and the bar,
  title, game over and arena each keep their own 16 px gutter, so content is
  361 px. App adds no gutter.
- **Both status regions stay mounted in every phase.** `FeedbackPanel`'s and
  the overflow message's live regions are each empty outside their own
  phase, so a verdict always fills a region that already exists. Before T114
  one region was always mounted; T114's first version inserted each region
  with its content, which many screen readers don't announce.
- **`OverflowControls` stays** as the overflow band's left cell and its
  status region (pre-flight ruling R3).
- **The rail cell holds its box through a discard.** It is placed by the
  band in every phase. A browser check in `viewport.spec.ts` showed the
  leaving rail tile jumping 22 px sideways before the fix.
- **"Hand · N of 5"** counts a tile lifted into the answer row as still in the
  hand, because its socket is held, not empty (product §1.10).
- **Motion:**
  - the streak glows on the answer tiles' edges (`.rim`, `.rimBright`) are
    gone, because B's table has no row for them; streak tiers are one to
    three chalk rings and the top tier's fan;
  - a miss draws one pink diagonal stroke per answer tile, which stays drawn,
    while the rings fade after drawing;
  - `nd-fire` plays on the `+` badge, so the tile doesn't move;
  - the arena's `@container` sideways clip is deleted, because B's exits move
    down. The slide-off sampling passes at 305, 320 and 375 without it, and
    fails once the old 46 px sideways exit is put back.
- **The three accessibility floors are exact counts.** The task file named
  two; the third had the same defect.
  - **The exact counts flaked on the random Practice deal.** The deal's
    answer length decides how many slots there are. Ten repeats of the two
    tests gave 11 failures in 20 runs, and `850b9f0`'s green run passed by
    chance.
  - **The fix:** each expected count now comes from the prompt's product,
    never from the controls counted. Thirty repeats of each test passed,
    and so did the whole browser suite five times over (305 runs).
- **The milestone review's fixes:**
  - **"Received"** lists the row's pay in printed order. It had listed the
    sorted hand's order, and after a discard it dropped the discarded tile.
  - **In overflow, the played tiles stay in their slots,** as plan ruling 3
    says. One `AnswerSlots` is mounted per round, so no slot moment replays.
  - **A miss's announcement** separates its two sentences.
- **Game over's row marks were 40 px right of centre.** The list kept the
  browser's default padding. This came in with M16, and screenshots caught
  it. A geometry check now holds the strip to the content box.
- **WebKit runs every spec, and a discard plays through to the next row.**
  Ori approved both on 2026-11-16, replacing plan ruling 15, under which
  WebKit ran only the fit sweep. Until then, M17's motion, rail and
  live-region checks ran in Chromium alone.
  - No test played a discard into the next row in a browser, because the
    gallery's `onNextRound` does nothing. The round moves on only when the
    exit, the perch drop and the verdict's celebration report their
    `animationend`, so an engine that never fired one would freeze the
    round.
  - `e2e/play.spec.ts` plays the real app on a seeded deal, with motion and
    under reduced motion: it answers row 1, discards, and expects row 2 with
    no input. A mutation observer records any Next Round in between; a
    retrying count had passed with Next Round rendered after the discard.
  - Each check failed on its break: auto-advance off fails the Row check in
    all four runs; the exit's animation set to `none` under reduced motion
    fails the reduced variant; Next Round rendered after a discard fails the
    record.

### Found, for Ori

- **Most of the work was never looked at.** Every check measures sizes,
  positions, counts and computed styles.
  - Two drawing bugs, dust under the tiles and chalk flecks on chalk, were
    caught only by reading the code.
  - Before the PR, Claude took Playwright screenshots of ten gallery states
    at 393 × 660 and looked at them. That found the off-centre row marks
    and the shortened "Received" line.
  - No phone has been used, and neither have iOS Safari or VoiceOver.
- **Below 375 px, after a discard, the result line squeezes its mark.** At
  320 and 305 px in English the ✓ shrinks to nothing, and at 305 px the text
  overlaps the rail cell by 1.9 px. Nothing scrolls sideways, and the word
  still carries the verdict. A fix needs a call: wrap the line, or shorten
  it.
- **Korean's Next button reads `다음 라운드`,** while the HUD now says `줄`
  (Row). The plan changed only the HUD's word.
- **The miss stroke** is one diagonal per tile. "A stroke across the answer
  tiles" could also mean one line across the row.
- **A screen reader hears "Correct" twice** across a discard: first with the
  instruction to discard, then with the round's result.
- **The dust specks** are dust on the chalk tile faces, `#aab3bf` on
  `#eef0ea`, low contrast. They are decorative and unmeasured.
