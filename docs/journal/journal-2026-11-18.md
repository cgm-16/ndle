Two bots reviewed PR #36 at `b8f07ba`: Codex left one finding and CodeRabbit

### Decisions

- **`e2e/play.spec.ts` is removed (Ori).**
  - AGENTS.md:56-60 and product.md §1.17 keep game behaviour out of the
    browser suite. A browser suite may assert only layout geometry,
    overflow and font metrics, and "must assert no game rule, reducer
    transition, or interaction flow". The rule dates from 2026-09-15 (#121).
  - The spec asserted the round's advance after a discard. Claude
    recommended it on 2026-11-16 without reading that rule, and CodeRabbit
    cited it.
  - WebKit still runs every spec, as Ori approved on 2026-11-16.
  - The round's `animationend` chain is covered by unit tests with fired
    events, and by the reduced-motion computed-style check.
  - Whether a real engine fires those events was observed only by the
    scratch probe of 2026-11-16, which played Chromium, desktop WebKit and
    iPhone 15 WebKit. A round on a real iPhone remains the manual check.
- **The verdict line wraps below 375 px (Ori).**
  - After a discard the line keeps clear of the rail cell. At 320 and 305 px
    it had no room: the ✓ shrank to 0 px in English, and to 17 px in Korean.
    At 305 px the text also overlapped the rail cell.
  - The mark no longer shrinks. The detail moves whole to a second line,
    under the word and beside the mark, inside the band's 150 px. At 375 and
    wider it stays one line, and §1.12's Feedback clause says so.
  - The first version of the fix lost the space before `·` at every width,
    and on a wrapped line it left the ✓ about 50 px from the word. Claude's
    screenshots caught both; the geometry checks had passed. Each now has
    a check that failed first.
- **The hand count includes a lifted tile (Claude, keeping M17's ruling).**
  - CodeRabbit read §1.12's "counts the seated tiles" as excluding a tile
    lifted into the answer row.
  - M17 counted it, because its socket stays held for it (product §1.10).
    That ruling went to Ori on 2026-11-16 among the decisions made without
    them.
  - A unit test now pins the lifted case in en and ko, and §1.12 says
    "the tiles in the sockets, a lifted tile's held socket included, and
    not the rail".
- **The verdict-line checks measure, not read text (CodeRabbit's
  re-review).**
  - **The space before `·`** is now a measured gap from the word to the
    dot. It is 7 px in both engines, and is 0 with the space gone. The
    check had read `innerText`, which the browser-suite rule doesn't allow.
  - **The 375 one-line check had compared the words' height with the
    panel's.** The panel grows with the words, so the check could not fail.
    It now checks that the detail starts on the word's line.
- **The flecks check reads the answer tiles at each seeked time.** The tiles
  bloom up to 4 px while the flecks fan out, so rects read once were stale.
